About

Research Interests

I am a researcher at the School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Changzhou University. My research focuses on developing deep learning methods for medical image analysis, with applications in disease diagnosis, lesion segmentation, and computer-aided detection systems.

My work spans retinal disease diagnosis via OCT imaging, skin lesion segmentation, brain tumor analysis, and diabetic retinopathy detection using lightweight convolutional neural networks. I am particularly interested in attention mechanisms, multi-scale feature fusion, and weakly supervised learning approaches for biomedical image understanding.
